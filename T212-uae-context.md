# Context Document: Trading 212 ME Limited (UAE / DIFC)

## 1. Corporate & Regulatory Profile
*   **Entity Name:** Trading 212 ME Limited (T212 ME).
*   **Location:** Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai, UAE.
*   **Regulator:** Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA).
*   **Prudential Category:** Category 2 (Matched Principal).
*   **Authorised Financial Services:** 
    *   Dealing in Investments as Principal (Matched Principal only).
    *   Dealing in Investments as Agent.
    *   Arranging Deals in Investments.
    *   Providing Custody.
*   **Endorsements:** 
    *   Retail Client Endorsement.
    *   Holding or Controlling Client Assets.

## 2. Business Model & Target Market
*   **Target Market:** 100% Retail Clients. The firm will exclusively onboard natural persons and will not accept Professional Clients, Market Counterparties, or institutional clients.
*   **Service Type:** Strictly execution-only brokerage. The firm provides no investment advice and no discretionary portfolio management. 
*   **Delivery Channel:** Services are delivered entirely via a proprietary mobile-first and web-based trading platform. Onboarding is fully digital.
*   **US Persons Policy:** Strict zero-tolerance policy for U.S. Persons (due to FATCA); the system automatically blocks onboarding if U.S. citizenship, birth, or address is detected.

## 3. Products & Services
The firm offers two distinct account types:
*   **T212 INVEST Account (Agency Model):** 
    *   Trading in exchange-traded securities including listed Equities, Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs), and complex Exchange Traded Products (ETPs / ETCs / Leveraged ETPs / Inverse ETPs). 
    *   Trades are executed on an agency basis via the group entity Trading 212 UK Limited. 
    *   Clients can also place uninvested cash into EU/UK-domiciled qualifying money market funds (QMMFs).
*   **T212 CFD Account (Matched Principal Model):** 
    *   Leveraged trading in Contracts for Difference (CFDs) on shares, ETFs, indices, futures, commodities, and currency pairs. 
    *   T212 ME acts as the counterparty to the client but hedges 100% of trades back-to-back with Trading 212 UK Limited, ensuring zero proprietary market risk.

## 4. Client Assets & Client Money (CASS)
*   **Client Money:** Segregated in dedicated, multi-currency "Client Money Accounts" held with **Barclays Bank PLC**. 
*   **Client Investments (Safe Custody):** T212 ME acts as the custodian for its clients, but utilises **Trading 212 UK Ltd** as the sub-custodian. T212 UK holds the assets in omnibus accounts with Third Party External Brokers (TPEBs) such as Interactive Brokers Ireland and BNY Mellon.
*   **Reconciliations:** The firm conducts daily internal and external reconciliations of client money and assets.

## 5. Key Systems, Controls & Compliance
*   **Client Classification:** All clients are defaulted to Retail Clients during the digital onboarding flow.
*   **Appropriateness vs. Suitability:** No suitability assessments are conducted (as no advice is given). However, the firm conducts mandatory **Appropriateness Assessments** (knowledge and experience tests) for clients wanting to trade CFDs and complex ETPs; failure results in denied access to those products.
*   **AML & KYC:** Onboarding utilises **Refinitiv World-Check One** for automated sanctions, adverse media, and PEP screening. The UAE National Risk Assessment rates the DIFC brokerage sector as medium-high risk for money laundering.
*   **Market Abuse:** The firm utilises **Eflow**, an automated transaction monitoring system, to detect market manipulation, insider trading, and abusive trading patterns, generating alerts for Compliance review.
*   **Complaints Handling:** Managed centrally via **Zendesk**, ensuring all communications are tracked, with acknowledgement required within 7 days and final resolution within 8 weeks.

## 6. Outsourcing & Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
The firm heavily leverages its group structure under a formal TPRM framework:
*   **Trading 212 Bulgaria (T212 BG):** Acts as the group's outsourcing hub, providing the proprietary trading platform, IT maintenance, customer support, AML/Fraud monitoring, and operational back-office support.
*   **Trading 212 UK Ltd (T212 UK):** Provides trade execution, sub-custody services, hedging for CFDs, and advisory support on IT governance and cybersecurity.
*   **Regulatory Standard:** The firm's Third-Party Risk Management policy aligns with rigorous standards (e.g., EBA Guidelines, DORA) and requires documented SLAs, KPIs, and regular due diligence.
