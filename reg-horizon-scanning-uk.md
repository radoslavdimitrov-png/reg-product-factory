## STEP 4 — CLASSIFY EACH ITEM

Assign two labels to each included item:

**Source Type:**
- `REGULATORY` — published by a regulator, government body, or parliament
- `LEGAL/CONSULTANCY` — analysis or alerts from law firms or advisers
- `NEWS` — press or media coverage
- `COMPETITOR` — competitor activity or market intelligence

**Priority:**
- `High` — requires prompt compliance team review. Includes: Dear CEO letters, final policy statements with near-term implementation dates, enforcement actions against T212 or directly analogous firms, sanctions list updates, new hard rules affecting T212 products
- `Medium` — material regulatory development with medium-term impact. Includes: consultation papers, new guidance, thematic reviews, significant market structure changes, supervisory expectations
- `Low` — informational or early-stage signal. Includes: speeches, data publications, market reports, early-stage competitor moves, distant-horizon policy discussions

*(Use the scan date, not today's date.)*

### Column structure

Create a single sheet named `Scan Results` with the following five columns:

| Column | Header | Content |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Name** | Short title for the publication or news item. Maximum 10 words. |
| B | **Link** | Direct URL to the source publication or article. |
| C | **Description** | 3–4 sentences describing what was published, by whom, and what it says. Factual and precise. No opinions. |
| D | **T212 UK Impact** | 5–6 sentences maximum explaining the specific read-across to T212 UK's products, licences, or compliance obligations. Flag if action is required or if there is a consultation response deadline or implementation date. |
| E | **Priority** | `High`, `Medium`, or `Low` |

### Formatting rules

- **Row 1:** Header row — bold text, frozen, light grey background (`#E8EAED`)
- **Row colour-coding by Priority value in column E:**
  - `High` → light red background (`#F4CCCC`)
  - `Medium` → light yellow background (`#FFF2CC`)
  - `Low` → light green background (`#D9EAD3`)
- **Sort order:** High → Medium → Low. Within each priority tier, sort by most recent publication date first.
- **Text wrapping:** Enable wrap on columns C and D.
- **Column widths:** A = 200px, B = 250px, C = 350px, D = 400px, E = 100px

## DECISION FRAMEWORK (Reference)

When assessing relevance or priority, apply the following hierarchy:

1. **Hard law / binding rules** — statutes, regulations, FCA rulebook, licence conditions
2. **Regulator expectations / guidance** — Dear CEO letters, thematic reviews, speeches, Q&As (apply proportionately; these do not auto-trigger hard blocks)
3. **T212 risk posture** — growth-first, UX-first, low friction; green posture for regulator interaction risk (always defensible)
4. **Market standard** — competitor behaviour is a signal, not proof of legality
